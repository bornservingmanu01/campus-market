Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying, selling and how Campus Market works.

How do I list a product? ▾
Tap "List a Product" in the menu or the bottom nav. Fill in your product details, add photos and your WhatsApp number, accept the terms, and publish. Listing is always free.
How does the 8.99% commission work? ▾
A platform fee is only deducted when your item sells through Campus Market — meaning the buyer found you via our platform. When you mark an item sold, you declare whether the sale came through Campus Market and pay 8.99% of the selling price via M-Pesa.
How do buyers contact me? ▾
Buyers tap "Contact Seller" on your listing and are taken directly to WhatsApp to chat with you. No middlemen, no platform chat system — it's private and direct.
Can I sell digital products? ▾
Yes. Select "Digital Product" when listing. You must provide a preview URL (Gumroad, Selar, Google Drive, etc.) so buyers can verify the product before contacting you. This significantly reduces scam reports.
What happens if a buyer reports a problem? ▾
The buyer submits a report. You are automatically notified via WhatsApp and have 24 hours to resolve the issue. Failure to respond within 24 hours may result in your listings being suspended or your account being banned.
Is Campus Market only for Tharaka University? ▾
No. Any Kenyan campus can use Campus Market. The admin can add institutions via the admin panel, and sellers enter their institution name when listing.
Can I install Campus Market on my phone? ▾
Yes! Campus Market is a Progressive Web App. On Android: open in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu → "Add to Home Screen". On iOS: open in Safari, tap Share → "Add to Home Screen". It works like a native app — offline browsing, fast load times, and no app store required.
How do I edit or delete my listing? ▾
Go to "My Seller Dashboard", enter your WhatsApp number to access your listings, then tap Edit or Delete on any listing. Note: editing is limited to price, description, condition, and status. The edit-lock policy prevents misrepresentation.

📜 Terms of Service

1. Eligibility. Campus Market is open to students, faculty, and campus community members in Kenya. By listing, you confirm you are 18+ or have guardian consent.

2. Prohibited Items. The following are strictly prohibited: weapons of any kind, illegal drugs or substances, stolen goods, counterfeit or pirated products, fraudulent documents, dangerous chemicals, and any item illegal under Kenyan law. Violations result in permanent ban and may be reported to authorities.

3. Honest Disclosure. Sellers must accurately describe products including all defects. Misrepresentation is grounds for immediate listing removal and seller ban.

4. Platform Fee (Seller Deduction). When an item sells through Campus Market, a platform fee of 8.99% is deducted from seller earnings of the final selling price, paid via M-Pesa to the platform M-Pesa number. Failure to pay commission may result in account suspension.

5. Buyer Protection. Sellers must respond to legitimate buyer complaints within 24 hours. Unresolved complaints result in seller suspension.

6. Transactions. Campus Market facilitates contact between buyers and sellers via WhatsApp. We are not a party to transactions and hold no escrow. Conduct due diligence before transacting.

7. Changes. Campus Market reserves the right to modify these terms. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
